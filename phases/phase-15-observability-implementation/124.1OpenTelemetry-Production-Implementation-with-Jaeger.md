


# 41. Head Sampling

Head sampling makes a decision near the beginning of the trace.

Example:

```text
100 requests
     |
     v
10% sampling
     |
     v
10 traces retained
```

Simple, but it may randomly discard an important error.

---

# 42. Tail Sampling

Tail sampling waits until enough information about the trace is available.

For example:

```text
Successful + fast
        ↓
sample normally

HTTP 500
        ↓
retain

Very slow
        ↓
retain

Critical operation
        ↓
retain
```

This is particularly useful for production troubleshooting.

The OpenTelemetry/Jaeger ecosystem supports tail-sampling components, although they increase Collector complexity and resource requirements. ([Jaeger][2])

---


The order request should not fail simply because tracing is unavailable.

The tracing pipeline should be designed as an observability dependency, not a business-critical synchronous dependency.
